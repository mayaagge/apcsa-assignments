Look at the sample subclass MyWarrior and compare it to your subclass. What is different? What is the same?
The subclass MyWarrior initializes the variable, caffeine for its players' special ability. It also include an instance variable of preferredLanguage which specifies what coding language is to be used. In the constructor, the MyWarrior subclass initializes their special ability and its max amount, whereas in our subclass, we initialize the max amount of special ability later on in the getSpecialMax() getter method. On top of that, in each of our methods, we account for the character running out of health (< 0) and render the player unable to perform actions. For the attack constructor, our subclass has a set amount of damage, whereas the attack in MyWarrior has randomized damage for 1-6.

How will your game interact with the user?
Our game interacts with the user mainly through a scanner. We first request for the player's name for their character and then request for their preferred class. With this information, we create their character and then randomize the fighter class for the enemy. After each action, the stats and actions of both players are printed to help keep the player up to date on the status of the game. When either player is defeated, the game will print a message specifying the winner.
